Privacy & terms

Last updated: November 2026. This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

Your files stay on your device

Documents and spreadsheets you open are read inside your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers or stored by us.

What is sent when you use AI features

When you ask a question, request a review, edit, quiz or report, the app sends your question and short excerpts of the relevant material (or computed statistics, for data) to our AI helper, which runs on Cloudflare Workers AI. We do not store this content or use it to train models. Cloudflare processes requests under its own privacy policy.

What we keep

We keep basic technical logs (such as errors and request counts) to keep the service running and to stop abuse. Your device may remember your voice and speed settings in local browser storage; you can clear this in your browser settings.

Voices and microphone

Read-aloud voices and voice input use your browser and device's built-in speech features, which may be processed by your browser or operating system provider.

Use responsibly

AI answers can be wrong. Check important facts against your source material. For exams, coursework and certifications, follow your institution's or exam body's rules on AI assistance; the writing tools give feedback and suggestions, and you remain responsible for your work.

Limits

The free service has daily and per-minute usage limits and may be unavailable at times. It is provided as-is, without warranty.
